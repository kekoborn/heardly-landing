Cookies and Analytics

Version of 22 October 2026

This is a translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Russian version, the Russian version prevails.

The website heardly.kekoborn.ru stores a small amount of data in your browser. Without your permission only the necessary entries are used; analytics is turned on only if you clicked “Accept” in the banner.

1. Necessary

Without them the website cannot remember your choice. They are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to us.

NamePurposeDuration
heardly-langthe website language you choseuntil you clear your browser data
heardly-cookie-consentyour choice in the cookie banner and the date of that choice12 months, after which the banner asks again

2. Analytics: Yandex Metrica

Only after “Accept”. Metrica (Yandex LLC, Russia) counts visits, shows which sections are read and where visitors leave, and records actions on the page (Session Replay, “Webvisor”). The e-mail field in the payment form is hidden from recordings. Yandex receives the IP address, browser and device information, the page address and the referral source. Metrica cookies:

NamePurposeDuration
_ym_uid, _ym_fa, i, yandexuid, yuidssdistinguishing one visitor from another1 year
_ym_ddate of the first visit1 year

_ym_isadwhether an ad blocker is present20 hours
_ym_hostIndexlimiting the number of requests1 day
_ym_metrika_enabledchecking that cookies were set60 minutes
_ym_visorcSession Replay operation30 minutes
yabs-sidvisit identifieruntil the browser is closed

Metrica may also set other technical cookies (for example, mdd). The full list is in Yandex Help.

3. How to opt out

Click “Decline” in the banner or change your decision here: . After you opt out, Metrica is not loaded. Metrica cookies that have already been set can be deleted in your browser settings.

4. Other external resources

The website’s fonts, images and scripts are loaded from the website itself. There are no third-party counters other than Metrica, and no advertising pixels or social media buttons. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (United States): like any hosting, it receives the visitor’s IP address in order to serve the page.

More about data processing — in the Personal Data Processing Policy.
