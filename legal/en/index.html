1. Product description

Heardly is a macOS program that turns speech into text right on your computer. The app lives in the menu bar. Speech recognition runs on the device: audio and transcript text are not sent to the internet.

A Heardly Pro subscription includes:

  • dictation into any app (the text is inserted where the cursor is);
  • live transcription of calls and meetings (microphone and computer audio) with no limit on minutes;
  • transcription of audio and video files (MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, MP4, MOV) with timestamps, with no limit on minutes;
  • an AI panel with a call summary in the live transcription window;
  • text export in all available formats (including PDF, DOCX and CSV), transcription history, and the heardly command in the terminal for connecting AI assistants;
  • updates and support for the entire subscription period.

System requirements: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 or later. Russian and 24 other European languages are recognised, including English. Internet access is needed to download the recognition model, receive updates and verify the subscription; transcription itself works offline.

The app is downloaded from the website free of charge and includes a free plan: unlimited dictation, live call transcription of up to 40 minutes per month, file transcription of up to 40 minutes per month (a separate limit), and export to TXT and SRT. The limits renew once a month. If the live transcription limit runs out during a call, recording continues for up to 20 more minutes, and the time used beyond the limit is deducted from the next month’s limit. On the free plan, a file longer than the remaining limit is not transcribed. A Heardly Pro subscription removes the limits and unlocks the additional features listed above.
