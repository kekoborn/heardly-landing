1. Operator

Self-employed citizen of the Russian Federation Ruslan Alyev (Алыев Руслан Мусаевич), INN 762706328316, e-mail support@kekoborn.ru.

2. Purpose

Taking out, paying for, providing, renewing and verifying a Heardly Pro subscription; connecting the subscription on my computers and restoring access; participation in promotions and the “Invite a Friend” program: issuing a referral code, applying the discount, crediting bonus days and notifying me about them; protection against abuse; refunds; replies to my requests.

3. Data covered

  • e-mail address;
  • technical device identifier, app version, time of subscription verification;
  • order and payment details: order number, plan, amount, date, status;
  • referral code and information about whose invitation a payment was made under and which bonuses were credited (the e-mail of one participant is not disclosed to the other);
  • IP address — only to limit the number of requests, stored for no longer than one hour;
  • one-time confirmation codes;
  • the content of my support requests.

4. Processing operations

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification, retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access), blocking, deletion and destruction. Processing is automated.

6. Term and withdrawal

This consent is valid for the subscription period and 3 years after it ends. I may withdraw it at any time by writing to support@kekoborn.ru with the subject “Withdrawal of consent”. After withdrawal, the operator stops processing and deletes the data within 30 days, except for information that must be retained by law. Without an e-mail address the subscription cannot be verified or restored, so after withdrawal it will no longer be possible to access Pro on new devices. After withdrawal of consent, the referral code stops working; unused bonus days remain valid until the end of the subscription period.
