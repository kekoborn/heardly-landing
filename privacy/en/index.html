1. Operator

The operator of personal data is Ruslan Alyev (Алыев Руслан Мусаевич), a self-employed citizen of the Russian Federation, INN 762706328316, e-mail support@kekoborn.ru (“we”). This Policy applies to the website heardly.pro and the Heardly app for macOS.

2. What data we process

  • E-mail provided when taking out a Heardly Pro subscription, receiving a gift, participating in the “Invite a Friend” program or restoring access.
  • Technical device identifier to which the licence is linked (a random string that reveals nothing about the contents of the computer), the app version and the time of the last subscription check.
  • Order and payment details: order number, plan, amount, date, status, and the date and version of the consent you gave. We do not receive or store bank card details: Robokassa processes them on its own page.
  • “Invite a Friend” program data: your referral code; for payments made by invitation, whose code was used, the discount amount and the bonus days credited. The buyer’s e-mail is not shown to the inviter, and vice versa.
  • IP address when the gift and invitation forms are used, to limit the number of requests from one address. Stored for no longer than one hour.
  • One-time confirmation codes that we send to your e-mail when a new computer is connected.
  • Support requests: the content of e-mail correspondence.
  • App crash reports: stack trace, app version and macOS version.
  • Website visit data (cookies, IP address, browser information and actions on the page) — only if you allowed analytics in the cookie banner. See Cookies and analytics for details.

Speech recordings, transcripts and files that you process in Heardly never leave your computer and are not sent to us. Crash reports do not include audio or transcript text.

3. Purposes of processing

  • entering into and performing the agreement: taking out, renewing and verifying a Heardly Pro subscription;
  • participation in promotions and the “Invite a Friend” program: issuing a code, applying the discount, crediting bonus days and notifying you about them;
  • protecting the website’s forms against abuse;
  • connecting the subscription on other computers and restoring access;
  • accepting payments, issuing receipts and making refunds;
  • replying to requests and technical support;
  • fixing app errors;
  • website visit statistics and improving the website (only with consent).

4. Legal basis

Processing is carried out on the basis of Article 6 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data”: your consent to the processing of personal data, given when taking out a subscription, receiving a gift or participating in the “Invite a Friend” program; consent to analytics, given in the cookie banner; and entering into and performing an agreement to which you are a party (the public offer).

5. Processing operations

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification, retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access), blocking, deletion and destruction. Processing is automated.

7. Retention periods

  • subscription and payment data — for the subscription period and 3 years after it ends (for requests and refund obligations), then deleted;
  • “Invite a Friend” program data — for the term of the program and 3 years after it ends;
  • IP addresses used to limit requests — up to 1 hour;
  • confirmation codes — 10 minutes;
  • support correspondence — up to 3 years from the last message;
  • analytics cookies — for the periods stated on the Cookies and analytics page.

If consent is withdrawn, the data is deleted within 30 days unless retention is required by law (for example, payment records for tax accounting).

8. Data protection

Data is transmitted only over secure channels (HTTPS). Only the operator has access to the subscription database.

9. Your rights

You have the right to obtain information about the processing of your data, to demand its correction, blocking or deletion, to withdraw your consent, and to appeal against our actions to Roskomnadzor (the Russian data protection authority) or in court. Write to support@kekoborn.ru with the subject “Personal data”. We will reply within 10 business days. Withdrawing consent to the processing of your e-mail means that the subscription can no longer be verified or restored.
